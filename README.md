# Hey, I'm Andre.

I'm a Brazilian independent developer and builder interested in software, technology, design, and automation.

I enjoy exploring ideas, understanding how things work, and building practical solutions to real-world problems. Much of what I do comes from curiosity, personal needs, or the belief that an existing solution could be simpler, more intuitive, or just better.

My interests span web development, software architecture, user experience, and the intersection of technology with everyday business operations.

### What I care about

- **Good design.** I believe software should be intuitive, consistent, and pleasant to use. Functionality and aesthetics aren't mutually exclusive.
- **Simplicity and maintainability.** I value straightforward solutions, readable code, and architecture that makes sense without unnecessary complexity.
- **Understanding how things work.** I like looking beyond the surface, questioning decisions, and learning what makes a system tick.
- **Practical problem-solving.** I prefer solutions that work reliably in the real world over technical complexity for its own sake.
- **Continuous learning.** I experiment, revisit assumptions, and regularly discover better ways to approach familiar problems.

### Technologies & interests

I currently work primarily with **TypeScript, React, TanStack, and PostgreSQL**, alongside various tools and services for building modern web applications.

I'm particularly interested in:

- Full-stack web development and software architecture
- UI/UX design and design systems
- Automation and API integrations
- Tools that make everyday work simpler and more efficient

### A little more

I'm detail-oriented, occasionally opinionated about interfaces, and probably spend more time refining things than strictly necessary.

I believe good software comes from balancing technical decisions with a clear understanding of the people and problems it's meant to serve.

>Still learning, still experimenting, and always looking for ways to improve what I build.
